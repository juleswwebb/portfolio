01 / THE EMBEDDED ARCHITECTURE

A short interrupt.
A clear data path.

The LSM6DS embedded pedometer raises a step-detection interrupt. The interrupt handler sets a flag; the foreground IMU task reads the counter over SPI and updates the local step model. Keeping bus work out of the interrupt makes the control flow easier to manage.

The first interrupt establishes a baseline so a stale sensor count is not treated as new activity after boot. A moving average smooths acceleration readings for the interface.

02 / THE HUMAN INTERFACE

Navigation with intent.

A joystick navigates steps, distance and goal progress on an SSD1306 OLED. Alternative units, test input, an adjustable goal, progress LEDs and a buzzer give the device a complete interaction flow.

The cooperative scheduler services the buttons, joystick and IMU at 50 Hz, with the display at 4 Hz. Goal notifications are latched as one-shot events, avoiding repeated alerts on every update.
