01 / THE CONTROL ARCHITECTURE

Requests become state transitions.

The controller handles internal car requests and external directional calls. It serves nearby compatible calls in its travel direction, reverses when no useful calls remain ahead, and updates the target only when the stopping distance allows it.

Calibration begins at the lower limit, zeros the high-speed encoder counter and records the floor-switch positions. Averaging each switch’s entry and exit positions establishes a repeatable floor centre.

02 / MOTION AND INTERLOCKS

Arrival is more than a position.

PI control brings the car to the target encoder count. A slew limit on motor command smooths acceleration. The ladder logic separates travel, safe-to-open and door-open states so a floor request cannot bypass the door interlock.

Doors open only when the car is at a floor and stationary. Open requests take priority, with a five-second dwell and a held-open condition before closing.

Control logic is a model of what can happen.

The project was tested in the simulator and on a scaled physical elevator. Translating the behaviour into explicit states made scheduling, motion and door logic easier to reason about and test together.
